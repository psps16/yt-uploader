Privacy Policy

Shorts Uploader · Last updated 15 November 2026

Shorts Uploader is a private tool that its owner runs on their own computer to upload videos to their own YouTube channels. It uses YouTube API Services. This policy explains what data it touches.

Data we access

  • Upload permission (youtube.upload): to upload videos the owner chooses.
  • Channel name and ID (youtube.readonly): to confirm a video is going to the right channel. No other account data, viewer data, comments or analytics are read.

How data is stored

The sign-in tokens Google issues are saved only on the owner's computer. The tool keeps a local log of uploads (date, channel, video ID and title). Nothing is sent to any server other than Google's.

Sharing

We do not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties. We do not use data for advertising.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time from your Google Account's security settings: security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. Deleting the tool's token files removes the saved sign-ins from the computer.

Google and YouTube

Shorts Uploader uses YouTube API Services. Using it means you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.
