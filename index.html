Shorts Uploader

A private tool that uploads short videos to its owner's own YouTube channels.

What it does. Shorts Uploader runs on its owner's computer. After the owner finishes a short animated video, the tool uploads it to one of the owner's YouTube channels (WhatIfology and Oddly Curious) with a title, description, tags and privacy setting, using the YouTube Data API.

Who it's for. Only the owner. It is not offered to the public and has no sign-up.

What it accesses. Permission to upload videos, and read-only access to the channel's name and ID so it can confirm each video goes to the right channel. Nothing else.
